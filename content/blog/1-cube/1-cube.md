## Emblem Cubes
Emblem cubes ask "How would the game be different if you started the game with ___ on the battlefield?" The most popular version of this concept, as far as I can tell, is Zennith's [cascade cube](https://cubecobra.com/cube/overview/cascadecube), where every player starts with a copy of {% hoverReveal "./b.jpg" %}Maelstrom Nexus{% endhoverReveal %} on the battlefield.
